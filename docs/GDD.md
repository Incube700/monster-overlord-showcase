# Monster Overlord · Game Design Document

**r9 playtest design** · Roblox · BurnHeartGames

You turn defeated enemies into an army. Winning the Crown lets you build that army from a tower, but bringing home a captured crystal means leaving the throne and risking the squad in the streets.

## First outing

A necromancer starts at a personal crypt with a staff and an empty squad. The first fight teaches three actions in order. Hit an enemy, collect its soul, and raise it. Give the recruit an attack order and see it land a hit. Stronger camps lead to specialist recruits, armor pieces, and new spells.

| Resource or tool | Decision for the player |
| --- | --- |
| Souls | Spend them to raise a recruit or save them for a stronger squad |
| Army slots | Fill the roster with Walkers or leave space for specialists |
| Armor pieces | Clear camps for a personal set and build up combat or movement bonuses |
| Spell pages | Choose abilities that work with the squad and terrain |
| Orders | Focus an enemy, regroup with the hero, or hold an area |

## Claim, rule, and raid

1. **Claim the Crown.** Defeat the central encounter, qualify for the ritual, and hold the Crown site for six seconds. The tower grows beneath the ruler.
2. **Prepare on the tower.** The rooftop throne adds one Walker every six seconds when there is space. Two spiral stairways connect the street and the rooftop. The ruler can defend from above or take the army out.
3. **Break a seal.** Choose a rival crypt. Protect the lich while it channels for eight seconds, then cross the opened threshold and take the crystal in person.
4. **Make it back.** Carrying the crystal marks the Overlord for rivals. The carrier must take a stairway to the summit. Other players can stop the lich, catch the carrier, or challenge the tower while the ruler is away.
5. **Choose the ending.** A crystal delivery unlocks the choice to ascend or keep ruling in r9. After the reign ends the Crown is available again.

The key playtest question is how long sitting on the throne feels productive before it starts to crowd out going on the offensive. Compare a quick raid with a raid that waits for recruits. Record the squad size at departure and return, the objective time, and the point where the defender first has a chance to react. [Playtest notes](PLAYTEST.md)

## Next prototype · Winged ascension

On reaching the crystal threshold, a portal opens above the tower. A beam gives the Overlord wings and the hero takes off through the portal. Back at the hub the wings remain visible, and a new gate offers the next outing.

Start with one crystal, matching the current r9 ending, then compare two crystals from different crypts. Beyond the new gate, start with two zombie encounters. The first gives the player a chance to raise a recruit, and the second lets them see how that recruit changes the fight. The threshold, wing persistence between servers, and the shape of the next region are open design decisions.
